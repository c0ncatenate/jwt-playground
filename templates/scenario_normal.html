Scenario 1 – Signed JWT flow

Log in as a user, issue a signed JWT, and inspect its header and payload. This is the "good" path and a baseline for the other scenarios.

  1. Pick a username (e.g. alice).
  2. Click Issue token.
  3. Look at the claims and hover over underlined terms to see what they mean.
